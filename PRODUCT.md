## Platform

## Stack

React 19 + Vite + TypeScript (same as the previous portfolio, user's choice). Animation via the `motion` package (motion.dev, formerly Framer Motion), explicitly requested by the user.

## Users

Two audiences, weighted equally:
- Recruiters and hiring leads scanning for a frontend / full-stack hire: they skim fast, checking stack, years, companies, and live work.
- Freelance clients deciding whether to commission a website or web app: they look at finished work and polish.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of Jomari Tiu, Frontend Engineer based in the Philippines (5+ years, since May 2021). Success = a visitor opens a live project or makes contact (email / LinkedIn / GitHub).

Agency craft plus product engineering in one person.
